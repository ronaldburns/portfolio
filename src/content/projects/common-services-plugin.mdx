## Common Services Plugin

This plugin is designed to integrate all the Online Services in the same style as CommonGame and CommonUser.
It is still in its VERY early stages and is not yet ready for production use.

### Features
  - **GetFriendsList**

<Img src="projects/common-services-plugin/Photos_VrlQRmEZ1f.png" alt="Get Friends List" />

  - **GetDisplayName**
    Automatically chooses the primary platform's name if accounts are linked, otherwise will choose the secondary platform name.
    Manuall platform selection is also available.
