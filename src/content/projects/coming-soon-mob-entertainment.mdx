I joined Mob Entertainment as a Gameplay Programmer in July 2024. The title undersells the scope a bit. Most of what I do sits somewhere between gameplay, engine tooling, and infrastructure.

## What I've been working on

A lot of my time goes into the Unreal Editor itself: custom editor modes, graphs, plugins, and tooling that cut down the repetitive parts of day-to-day development. The less the editor fights you, the more time the team spends actually building the game.

On the runtime side I'm writing a lot of C++, mostly gameplay systems that need to scale and stay fast under load. Mass Entity has been central to that. It's what we use to manage large numbers of game objects without the per-actor overhead you'd normally run into.

Some of the specific systems I've built or worked on:

- A save system that reliably persists player progress.
- Multiplayer through Epic Online Services, including matchmaking and voice chat.
- Menus and in-game UI that need to stay responsive.
- Core mechanics like movement and inventory.

## Tooling and DevOps

Outside of gameplay code, a fair amount of my time goes to the build and deployment pipeline. I've customized Submit Tool, Unreal Game Sync, and Horde to fit how our team actually works, and I help out on the DevOps side to keep builds and deploys moving.

The stack I touch most:

- Unreal Engine and C++
- Epic Online Services
- Perforce for version control
- Jenkins for builds
- Jira and Confluence
- Terraform, Nomad, Vault, Kubernetes, and Docker on the infrastructure side

## The stuff I can't talk about

There's also a chunk of work on unannounced projects I can't get into yet. NDAs. What I'll say is it's been some of the more interesting gameplay work I've done.

That's the short version. If you want to talk shop about Unreal, tooling, or game dev in general, I'm around.
